How a site earns authorship

  1. Measure. A site team runs an eddy-covariance tower and processes its measurements.
  2. Share openly. The team submits its data to a regional network under a CC BY 4.0 license, so anyone can reuse them with credit.
  3. Join FLUXNET. The data are processed with the common ONEFlux pipeline and published through the FLUXNET Shuttle.
  4. Co-author the paper. The site's team is offered co-author slots on the author list of the annual data paper, set by the table alongside.

CC BY 4.0 lets anyone reuse the data as long as they credit the people who collected it and follow the data use policy. This paper is designed to give credit to every team that shares their data through FLUXNET.

Author slots per site

A site's co-author slots cover its whole team, PI included. Longer records and more recent data earn more co-author slots. A year counts if it has at least one month of processed data.

About these numbers

  • To respect co-authors' privacy, this page shows no names. Progress is reported for regional networks, never for individual sites.
  • Each co-author counts once, even when more than one site names them. Confirming means checking one's name, affiliations and contact details. Before submission, every co-author will also see the full draft and author list and be asked to consent to inclusion.
  • Site-years count the years, at each site, with at least one month of processed data.
